### Service 2 — Temperature & Humidity Chamber

- **Category:** Environment
- **Accent:** Panasonic Blue (`#00AEEF`)
- **Icon:** Thermometer + water droplet

### Service 3 — Thermal Shock Chamber

- **Category:** Environment
- **Accent:** Panasonic Blue (`#00AEEF`)
